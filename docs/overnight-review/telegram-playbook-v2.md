# Telegram playbook v2 (France)

> Draft for discussion. No Telegram settings were changed. Any earlier playbook ("v1") was not available in this workspace, so this version is written from the current strategy and prototype.

**Objective:** grow the Vision X1 community in France.
**Role of Telegram:** the conversion and community destination. The website (Match Pages, Track Record) is the trust layer and the source of truth.
**Language:** French on Telegram. Examples below are in French with English notes.

## 1. Channel positioning

**One line:** *L'analyse foot, avant le coup d'envoi.* ("Football analysis, before kickoff.")

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Football analysis: data, AI summary, a named analyst's View | A "pronos" / tips channel |
| Published **before** kickoff, kept in public, including when wrong | A channel that deletes misses or edits after the match |
| A daily habit: briefing, line-ups, the View, the debrief | An odds, stakes or "bankroll" feed |
| 18+, education and context | A promise of gains |

**Structure (recommended):** a **channel** for broadcasts plus a **linked discussion group** for the community. Comments under each post keep the channel clean while enabling discussion. (Needs an admin decision; not changed.)

## 5. First 7 days (onboarding experience)

New members join at random times, so this is a **content rhythm**, not a drip sequence (a public channel cannot message individuals). Optionally, a welcome bot could DM a short guide; that requires an admin/bot decision.

| Day | Theme | Post | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Who we are** | Pinned message + "Comment lire une page match en 30 secondes" (3 layers, 1 image) | Understand the product |
| D2 | **The method** | "Pourquoi on verrouille nos analyses avant le match" + Track Record link | Trust |
| D3 | **First matchday** | Full matchday rhythm (section 7) | Habit |
| D4 | **Meet the analyst** | Short intro of one analyst: league, style, how they set conviction | Human face |
| D5 | **When we were wrong** | One honest debrief of a missed View: what the data said, what happened | Credibility |
| D6 | **Your turn** | Poll: "Quel match du week-end voulez-vous qu'on analyse ?" | Participation |
| D7 | **Weekly recap** | Week in Views: published / before kickoff / edits = 0, links to every page | Accountability habit |

Repeat D1-style "how to read a match page" content every 2–3 weeks for new joiners.

## 7. Matchday rhythm

| Time | Post | Content |
|---|---|---|
| ~09:00 | ☀️ Briefing | Section 6 |
| H-8 to H-3 | 🔒 **Vision X1** | Section 8. Published on the site first, then here. |
| H-1 | 📋 **Compos** | Confirmed XIs, what changes vs the View's assumptions (no new read, no edit). |
| H-0 | ⚽ Coup d'envoi | Optional: one tactical thing to watch. Discussion opens in the group. |
| Half-time | (group only) | Community discussion; no new reads from the brand. |
| FT + 1 h | 🌙 **Debrief** | What happened vs the View; "read held / missed" stated plainly; link to the Track Record entry. |

Quiet hours: no brand posts after 23:00 or before 08:00.

## 8. Vision X1 View format (Telegram)

> 🔒 **Vision X1 · PSG – OM** · Ligue 1 · 21h
>
> **PSG ont l'avantage**
> Conviction : ●●○ moyenne
>
> **Pourquoi** : PSG contrôlent le ballon (66 % de possession à domicile) ; la meilleure arme de l'OM reste la transition.
> **Le risque principal** : défenseur central incertain, compos à H-1.
>
> ✍️ Camille Rousseau · publiée à 8h15 · **verrouillée** (réf. VX1-26-0412)
> 📄 Data, résumé IA et risques complets : [lien page match]
> 🔞 18+ · Analyse, pas de conseil de pari

Rules:
- Same wording as the website (analytical: *"ont l'avantage"*, *"profil plus solide"*). Never "gagnant", "sûr", "banco", "à jouer".
- Always the record ID and publication time, so the Telegram post points to the locked record.
- Never post a View on Telegram that isn't on the site.
- Never edit or delete a View post. Corrections go in a new message.

## 9. Interaction prompts

Use one or two a day, in the group or as polls:
- *"Quel duel va décider PSG – OM ?"* (poll: 3 options)
- *"Vous êtes d'accord avec la Vision X1 du jour ? Pourquoi ?"*
- *"Quelle stat vous a surpris cette semaine ?"*
- *"Quel match du week-end mérite une page complète ?"*
- *"Debrief : qu'est-ce qu'on a mal lu ?"* (after a missed View)

Avoid prompts about bets placed, stakes, odds or winnings.

## 10. Community habits we want

1. **Open the briefing every morning**: a daily reason to check in.
2. **Read the page before kickoff**: Telegram sends people to the trust layer.
3. **Discuss the football**: duels, line-ups, tactics.
4. **Check the debrief**: the honesty ritual that builds trust over months.
5. **Suggest matches**: members shape coverage.

## 11. Moderation principles

| Rule | Action |
|---|---|
| No promotion of other tips channels, betting links, referral codes or "VIP" offers | Delete + warn; ban on repeat |
| No posting of bet slips, stakes or winnings screenshots | Delete with a short explanation ("on parle foot, pas mises") |
| No harassment, discrimination or club hate | Delete + mute/ban |
| Minors | Ban; the community is 18+ |
| Signs of problem gambling in messages | Private, non-judgemental message with support resources (joueurs-info-service.fr). Never mock. |
| Disagreement with a View | Welcome. Answer with data, never delete criticism. |

Bots: anti-spam plus link filtering in the group. Two or more human moderators on matchdays.

## 12. Retention mechanisms

- **Rituals over volume:** 3–5 predictable posts a day beat 20 random ones.
- **Weekly accountability recap** (Sunday evening): Views published, all before kickoff, 0 edits; link to the Track Record.
- **Monthly "what we learned"**: one honest look at the analysts' calibration (performance as context, never as a promise).
- **Analyst presence:** the analyst answers 2–3 questions after their View.
- **Member input:** monthly poll on which leagues and matches to cover.
- **Season arcs:** "Le Classique", "Course au titre": recurring series people follow.
- **Don't over-notify:** 1–2 posts a day with notifications on; the rest silent.

## 13. Hard lines (non-negotiable)

- No guaranteed outcomes, profits or "easy money".
- No "beat the bookmaker", no odds-comparison, no stake sizing, no bankroll content.
- No certainty language ("sûr", "garanti", "100 %", "lock").
- 18+ statement in the bio, the pinned message and every View post.
- Everything on Telegram must be consistent with the public Track Record.
