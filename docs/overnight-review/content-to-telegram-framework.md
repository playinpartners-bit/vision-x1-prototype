# Content → Telegram framework

**Chain:** football content (TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube Shorts) → `#/welcome` (or a Match Page) → Telegram.
**Rule:** football-first, trust-first. Content earns attention with football insight; the landing earns trust; Telegram earns the habit.
**Never:** guaranteed results, "pronos sûrs", gains, odds, bankroll, "VIP".

Examples are in French (target audience), with English notes.

## 2. Five content formats

| Format | Length / shape | Best hooks | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The Stat Short** | 15–30 s vertical video, 1 stat + 1 visual from the Match Page | 1, 2, 10 | Landing (`#/welcome`) |
| **B. The Locked View** | Screen recording of the Match Page: data → AI → View → "verrouillée" timestamp | 3, 7 | Match Page |
| **C. The Honest Debrief** | Talking head or carousel the day after: what we read, what happened | 4, 8 | Track Record → Telegram |
| **D. The Matchday Guide** | Carousel (5 slides): 3 things to watch + key duel + risk | 5, 6 | Landing |
| **E. The Line-up Flash** | Story / short post at H-1 | 9 | Telegram directly (already-warm audience) |

## 3. Five CTA angles (end of content)

Soft, informational, no outcomes.

| Angle | CTA (FR) | English |
|---|---|---|
| **See the full analysis** | *« La page complète du match est en lien. »* | The full match page is in the link. |
| **Before kickoff** | *« Notre lecture est publiée avant le coup d'envoi : le lien est en bio. »* | Our read is published before kickoff; link in bio. |
| **Check us** | *« Toutes nos analyses sont horodatées. Vérifiez-nous. »* | Every analysis is timestamped. Check us. |
| **Daily habit** | *« Le briefing foot de chaque matin est sur notre Telegram. »* | The daily football briefing is on our Telegram. |
| **Join the discussion** | *« On en débat sur Telegram : lien en bio. »* | We're discussing it on Telegram; link in bio. |

Rule of thumb: **trust angles (1–3) → landing; habit angles (4–5) → Telegram.** A cold viewer goes to the landing first; a returning viewer can go straight to Telegram.

## 4. Content → landing transitions (examples)

| Content | Last frame / caption | Link (UTM) | What the landing must show immediately |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · Stat Short on PSG possession | *« La page complète du match est en lien. »* | `utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social_organic&utm_campaign=classique_2026_10&utm_content=psg_possession_stat` | The **same match** in the hero (PSG vs Marseille) and the **same stat** visible in the Data layer card |
| B · Locked View recording | *« Publiée 12h avant. Verrouillée. »* | `...&utm_content=psg_om_locked_view` | Hero match card with "Locked 12 h 45 min before kickoff" |
| C · Honest debrief | *« Toutes nos analyses sont horodatées. Vérifiez-nous. »* | `...&utm_content=debrief_lyon_lille` → Track Record | Track Record accountability numbers first |
| D · Matchday guide carousel | Slide 5: *« Analyse complète + risques : lien en bio »* | `...&utm_medium=bio_link&utm_content=guide_j8` | The match from the carousel in the hero |

**Continuity rule:** the first screen of the landing must match the content the person just watched (same match, same claim). If it doesn't, the visitor feels baited and bounces. This is why the landing's fixture must be **configurable per campaign** (see landing-audit, "Before real traffic").

## 5. Landing → Telegram transition

1. **Hero:** match + promise. No Telegram. ("Explore the match")
2. **Layers + preview:** understand the product.
3. **Accountability:** published / locked / kept in public. This is the trust gate.
4. **Telegram band (value-led):**
   > *« Chaque matin : les matchs du jour. Avant le coup d'envoi : la Vision X1 verrouillée. Après les compos : ce qui change. »*
   > **[Rejoindre Vision X1 sur Telegram]**
5. **Final section:** *Explore the match* (primary) · *Rejoindre sur Telegram* (secondary; recommended in place of the demo "Create free account").
6. **Match Page bottom** (most phone visitors arrive here via the sticky CTA): *« Recevoir les alertes du match sur Telegram »* as the card's primary.

## 7. Guardrails checklist (every piece)

- [ ] Football insight first; the CTA is the last 10 %.
- [ ] No "sûr", "garanti", "gains", "cote", "mise", "banco", "VIP".
- [ ] Mentions 18+ where the Vision X1 View is shown.
- [ ] Any number shown is real and matches the Match Page.
- [ ] Link carries full UTMs (see growth-measurement-plan §4).
- [ ] The landing's first screen matches the content.
